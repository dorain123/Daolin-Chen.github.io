你好，我是陈道林，上海交通大学电子与计算机工程专业本科生。我对 AI Agent 保持长期兴趣，尤其关注智能体如何利用工具、检索证据、理解多模态信息，并在真实环境中可靠地完成任务。

我的实践涵盖移动 GUI 智能体的数据采集与评测、科研论文的多模态 Agentic RAG，以及大语言模型与贝叶斯优化在能源和化工场景中的应用。我希望把算法研究与系统实现结合起来，构建可验证、可复现的智能系统。

I am Daolin Chen, an undergraduate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My long-term interest is in AI agents: how they use tools, retrieve evidence, understand multimodal information, and reliably complete tasks in real-world environments.

My work spans data collection and evaluation for mobile GUI agents, multimodal agentic RAG for scientific papers, and applications of LLMs and Bayesian optimization to energy and chemical engineering. I aim to connect algorithmic research with system building to develop verifiable, reproducible intelligent systems.

AutoSearch：交互驱动的移动 GUI 智能体轨迹自动采集AutoSearch: Interaction-Driven Mobile GUI Trajectory Collection

第一作者 · 2026.04 — 至今First author · Apr. 2026 — Present

提出面向模拟器与真实设备的自动化采集框架。结合 Explorer–Decider 与进度感知 DFS，从截图、UI 层级与历史交互生成目标并执行动作；通过状态转移验证、回溯与完整路径重放，保留连贯且经过验证的交互轨迹。

Proposed an automated collection framework for emulators and real devices. An Explorer–Decider pipeline with progress-aware DFS generates goals from screenshots, UI hierarchies, and interaction history, then grounds them into actions. Transition verification, backtracking, and full-path replay retain coherent, verified trajectories.

关键结果KEY RESULT7 个应用 · 28,228 条轨迹 · 23,577 条完整路径。7 apps · 28,228 trajectories · 23,577 complete paths.

自动评审与盲测人工评分的相关性：Pearson r = 0.961；Spearman ρ = 0.868。Automatic judgments vs. blind human ratings: Pearson r = 0.961; Spearman ρ = 0.868.
